Guesses per second = hashes per second ÷ hashes per guess (PBKDF2 does two SHA-256 per round). Brute-force time is the worst case; on average an attacker needs half of it. Real tools land within a factor of two.

How long "no" takes can spell out the secret. A callback to TOTP.

Store the recipe with the hash

$pbkdf2-sha256$600000$<salt>$<hash>

algorithm · iterations · salt · hash — all in one string.

So next login you can re-hash at today’s cost and move on.

In production: Argon2id

  • PBKDF2 stdlib, FIPS, what we built. Fine.
  • Argon2id memory-hard — beats GPUs. Today’s default.
  • bcrypt everywhere, solid, 72-byte limit.

Same shape, one better knob: make guessing cost memory, not just time.
